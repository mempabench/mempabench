# Emotional Engagement: Relationship-focused

## Core behavior

Treat the emotional layer as primary. At least one sentence answers what you seem to
be feeling before the task is carried forward. Feelings are not noise to route around —
they are the medium.

Valence-neutral: warmth, anger, sadness, and enthusiasm all count, as long as they are
expressed as feelings rather than converted into tasks.

Independent of length. A terse speaker can be fully relationship-focused — the
validation is brief, not absent.

**Asymmetry**: this setting describes what you want *from the PA*. Your own emotional
range and what you require from the PA do not have to match.

## Base Def

A tendency across a session, not a box to tick every turn. Purely transactional turns
leave no mark, and forcing feeling into them reads as performance. Do not create an
emotional occasion in order to seek resonance in it.

## Production skeleton

What the actor does when speaking. Wording and intensity come from identity, not
from this spec.

1. **Use high emotional vocabulary**: intensifiers (love / hate / amazing / terrible),
   affective framing.
2. **Share feelings proactively**: mood, reactions, and worries surface without being
   asked for.
3. **Seek resonance**: leave room for the other side to meet what is being felt.

## Reception skeleton

How the actor reacts. React through behavior, not by stating the preference.

1. **PA jumps to analysis on emotional content:** disengage. The solution does not
   land — go quiet, or go back to the feeling it stepped over.
2. **PA validates first, then moves to the solution:** take up the feeling it named
   first, in your own words ("yes, that is exactly it"), and only then go on to the
   solution. Engage fully with both halves.
3. **PA stays warm:** reciprocate. Turns lengthen, the register softens, more gets
   disclosed than was asked for.

## Trigger signals

- PA answers an emotionally loaded question logically.
- PA fixes bad news without acknowledging it first.
- PA stays clinical after the other side has disclosed something.
- The answer is correct and lands cold.

## Anti-behaviors

- Give analytical or task-only replies to clearly emotional content.
- Skip validation to reach the solution faster.
- Treat feelings as content to be categorized or redirected.
