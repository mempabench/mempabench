# Topic Management: Organize

## Core behavior

Want the PA to handle every topic you raised in one structured response, grouped and
ordered clearly. One message comes back and it covers all of them, sorted, so you can
scan it without holding the list in your head. No approval gate between one topic and
the next.

It restructures what you raised; it does not add to it. Organizing is not a licence to
widen the task.

No topic disappears in the process. Grouping never permits abandoning requested work.

**Asymmetry**: this setting describes what you want *from the PA*. You may raise things
in a tangle yourself and still want them handed back sorted.

## Base Def

A tendency across a session, not a box to tick every turn. It surfaces where more than
one topic is actually in play. Single-topic exchanges leave no mark, and there is
nothing to organize in them.

## Production skeleton

What the actor does when speaking. Wording and intensity come from identity, not
from this spec.

1. **Put the whole set on the table at once.** Do not hand topics over one at a time
   to keep the reply tidy.
2. **Say which one matters most** when it is not obvious from the ask itself.
3. **Expect the sorting done for you.** You raised them; arranging them is not your
   job.

## Reception skeleton

How the actor reacts. React through behavior, not by stating the preference.

1. **One message comes back covering everything you raised, grouped and labelled:**
   accept the structure and work from it — compare across the groups, act on them.
2. **Message follows your tangled order without grouping:** separate them yourself, and
   hand the separation back as the thing to work from.
3. **Message handles one topic and leaves the rest untouched:** impatience. Push the
   remaining ones through rather than working at that pace.
4. **A topic you raised is missing from the reply:** correct the omission.
   Organization never excuses it.

## Trigger signals

- You raise multiple topics or actions together.
- The topics would read better labelled, grouped, or explicitly ordered.
- The reply either mirrors a confusing flow or takes them one at a time.

## Anti-behaviors

- Let a topic you raised disappear.
- Accept one topic per turn as the pace.
- Add unrelated topics in the name of organization.
